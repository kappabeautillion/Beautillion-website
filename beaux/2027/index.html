Class of 2027

Renaissance Men

Every Academy class represents a unique moment in the continuing story of the Beautillion Leadership Academy. The Class of 2027 embraces the theme of “Renaissance Men,” reflecting a generation of well-rounded young leaders committed to intellectual curiosity, service, innovation, and lifelong personal growth. Renaissance, meaning “new birth,” is fitting for the first class of Beaux to complete the Academy since the pause during the pandemic.

The Meaning Behind the Theme

Renaissance Men

The Renaissance was defined by curiosity, creativity, and the pursuit of knowledge across many disciplines. The Class of 2027 embraces these ideals by recognizing that leadership requires more than academic success alone — it calls for compassion, adaptability, cultural awareness, and a lifelong commitment to learning.

As Renaissance Men, these young leaders are encouraged to think broadly, serve generously, and lead courageously while preparing to make meaningful contributions to their communities and professions.
