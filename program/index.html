The Program

A Year of Deliberate, Structured Growth

From orientation to the presentation ceremony, every phase of the Academy is designed to build a specific set of skills and character traits.

Curriculum

Four Phases, One Journey

Phase One — Orientation & Etiquette

Beaux and their families meet the committee, review expectations, and begin foundational training in professional and social etiquette, grooming, and personal presentation.

Phase Two — Leadership Development

Workshops in public speaking, goal-setting, conflict resolution, and self-discipline, often led by alumni and community professionals.

Phase Three — Financial Literacy

Sessions on budgeting, credit, saving, and long-term financial planning, taught in partnership with local financial institutions.

Phase Four — Service & Presentation

Structured community service projects culminate in the formal Beautillion presentation, where each Beau is formally recognized before family and community.

What to Expect

Commitment & Structure

  • Weekly or bi-weekly sessions throughout the program year
  • One-on-one mentorship pairing for every Beau
  • A minimum number of required community service hours
  • Regular family communication and progress updates
  • A formal rehearsal series ahead of the presentation ceremony

Committee note: update the specifics above (session cadence, service hour minimums) to match your chapter's actual program calendar.
