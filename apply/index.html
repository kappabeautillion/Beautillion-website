Apply to the Academy

Your Leadership Journey Begins Here.

The Beautillion Leadership Academy is a comprehensive leadership-development experience designed to prepare young men for success in college, career, and community.

Through mentorship, professional development, service, cultural enrichment, college preparation, and meaningful relationships, participants build the confidence, character, and skills necessary to lead with purpose.

We invite motivated young men and their families to apply for the next Academy class.

Who Should Apply

We Are Looking for Young Men Ready to Grow

The Academy welcomes young men who demonstrate academic potential, good character, curiosity, commitment, and a willingness to grow.

Applicants do not need to arrive as polished public speakers or experienced leaders. They should arrive prepared to participate fully, learn from accomplished mentors, serve their communities, and challenge themselves throughout the Academy experience.

What to Expect

The Participant Experience

01

Mentorship

Each participant is paired with mentors and professionals who provide guidance regarding college preparation, career exploration, leadership, and personal development.

02

Academy Sessions

Participants attend interactive Academy sessions focused on leadership, financial literacy, communication, professional presence, college readiness, service, networking, and personal growth.

03

Community and Cultural Experiences

The Academy includes service activities, cultural experiences, professional exposure, and opportunities to build meaningful relationships with peers and mentors.

04

Scholarships and Recognition

Participants may be eligible for scholarships and Academy honors based on the applicable criteria established for their class year.

05

The Beautillion Gala

The Academy culminates in the Beautillion Gala, where participants are formally recognized and celebrated before their families, mentors, supporters, and community.

Participation expectations, attendance requirements, fundraising responsibilities, scholarship criteria, gala obligations, and participant costs may vary by Academy class. Current requirements will be provided before enrollment and must be acknowledged by the participant and parent or guardian.
