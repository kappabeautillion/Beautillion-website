Applications Open

Begin Your Application

Spaces in each Beautillion class are limited. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, so we encourage you to apply early.

Eligibility

Who Should Apply

  • Young men currently enrolled in high school (grades to be specified by your chapter)
  • A minimum GPA as set by the committee
  • Willingness to commit to the full program calendar
  • Interest in leadership, service, and personal growth
The Process

Submit Application

Complete the form with your information and a short essay response.

Committee Review

Our committee reviews every application and reaches out to schedule an interview.

Interview

A brief conversation with the committee to learn more about you and your goals.

Welcome to the Academy

Accepted Beaux and their families are invited to orientation to begin the program.
